Sonar vs Radar

Both do the exact same thing: send a wave, time the echo, turn time into distance. The only real difference is the wave — and sound is about a million times slower than radio. That one fact decides all the hardware.

Wave

Soundpressure wave in air — needs air to travel
Radiolight's cousin — works in a vacuum, fog, rain

Where each wins

Short range, indoors, underwatersubs use sonar — radio dies in water
Long range, all weather, speedsound is useless past a few meters of air

The twist: your Phase 1 "radar" is technically a sonar — it pings with sound. But the math, the sweep, the detection code, and the tracking are identical to real radar. That's the whole idea: learn the hard parts on the easy wave, then swap in real RF for Phase 2.
